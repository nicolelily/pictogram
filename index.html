2,000 Beagles at Ridglan Farms

#30DayChartChallenge 2026 — Day 2: Pictogram
On March 15, 2026, over 100 activists entered Ridglan Farms in Blue Mounds, Wisconsin and openly rescued 30 beagles bred for laboratory experimentation. Eight of those dogs were seized back by the sheriff's office and returned to the facility. 22 are now safe with loving families. The remaining 1,970 beagles are still inside. On April 19, 2026, organizers plan to return with 2,000 rescuers — one for every dog. Read the plan →
